**Content freeze**

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Omar Hosam
- Creative North Star: "The Creator Broadcast Pipeline"
- Intact mark: terminal dot + two rising orange slashes
- Type: Syne / IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Mono
- **The One Signal Rule:** kinetic orange only for live state, progress, one primary action, or deliberate interruption
- Flat by default; no gradients, glass, or decorative shadows

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over hype
2. One signal — orange is kinetic meaning
3. Systems craft — calm surfaces so authored moments stay recognizable
4. Founders first — freelance / consult / training clear in five seconds
5. Reduced-motion settle for all ambient motion

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast on shadcn pairs
- 44px minimum interactive targets
- Visible kinetic-orange focus
- `prefers-reduced-motion` must settle, not decorate
